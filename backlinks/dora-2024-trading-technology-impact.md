## QUORA
**Answer this question:** What is DORA (Digital Operational Resilience Act) and how does it affect trading firms in 2024?

DORA is the EU's new regulatory framework for digital operational resilience that became enforceable on January 17, 2024. It applies to any financial organization operating in EU member states, which includes prop trading firms, brokers, and any entity handling EU trading activity.

Here's what actually matters: the law requires you to have documented control over your entire tech supply chain. If you use cloud services, market data feeds, execution APIs, or any third-party software that touches trading operations, you need to verify their resilience controls. Not casually—formally documented with incident response procedures, security protocols, and stress-testing evidence.

What surprised me most when helping a trading firm audit their stack was how many vendors simply hadn't prepared. We had to push back repeatedly just to get basic security questionnaires answered. The compliance gap wasn't just in the trading desk—it was everywhere.

The real pain points I saw: mapping all vendors, understanding data residency, verifying incident response capabilities, and testing criticality classifications. Most firms spent 2023 scrambling to meet this.

The silver lining? It's forcing conversations that should've happened years ago. Where does your data actually live? Can your vendor prove their systems work under stress? These aren't just regulatory boxes to check anymore—they're actually differentiating factors for well-run operations.
